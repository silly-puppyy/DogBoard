# DogBoard privacy policy

DogBoard is a keyboard. Everything you type stays on your phone. DogBoard has no account, no analytics, no ads and no
crash reporting service: nothing you type, and nothing about how you type, is ever sent anywhere.

## What stays on your phone

- The words DogBoard learns from your typing, the words you add or block, and your text replacements.
- Your clipboard history, when you turn it on.
- Your themes and settings.

You can see and delete what DogBoard learned in Settings › Your words, and export or remove it at any time.
Uninstalling DogBoard deletes all of it.

## When DogBoard uses the internet

DogBoard connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Language downloads.** When you add a language, DogBoard downloads its dictionary from DogBoard's releases on
  GitHub (github.com/silly-puppyy/DogBoard-packs), and checks now and then for a newer version of the dictionaries you
  have. These requests contain no information about you or your typing. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any
  download; see GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Expand short links** (Settings › Clipboard, off by default). When you copy a short link from a known link
  shortener (bit.ly, t.co, pin.it and a few others), DogBoard asks that shortener where the link leads, so it can paste
  the full, cleaned link. Only the copied link is sent, to that shortener only, without cookies.
- **Link previews** (Settings › Clipboard, off by default). For links in your clipboard history, DogBoard fetches the
  page once to show its title and picture. Only the request for that page is sent, without cookies.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: only for the cases above.
- **Contacts** (optional, off until you allow it): to suggest contact names and their details while you type. Contacts
  are read on the phone and never sent anywhere.
- **Notifications**: to tell you if DogBoard stopped because of an error. The error report is shown to you; DogBoard
  never sends it anywhere by itself.
- **Vibration**: for key-press feedback.
