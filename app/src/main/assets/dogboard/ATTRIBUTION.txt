DogBoard word data

en.lexicon.tsv (built by tools/data/build_lexicon.py) is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), because it contains frequencies from wordfreq.
It is built from:

  SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) 2020.12.07, http://wordlist.aspell.net/
    The collective work is Copyright 2000-2018 by Kevin Atkinson and the contributors listed in
    its copyright notice; used under that notice, included in full in licenses/SCOWL-Copyright.txt.
  wordfreq 3.1.1 word frequencies, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, data CC BY-SA 4.0,
    by Robyn Speer and contributors, which draw on Wikipedia, OpenSubtitles (via OPUS),
    SUBTLEX, NewsCrawl, GlobalVoices, Google Books Ngrams, Reddit and Twitter text.
  Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org), CC0: names of people, companies, brands, websites, apps.
  US Census Bureau name frequency lists (1990 first names, 2010 surnames), public domain.
  GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), CC BY 4.0: names of cities, countries and regions.
  Wikipedia "Lists of common misspellings/For machines", CC BY-SA 4.0: used only to leave
    misspellings out.

en.bigrams.tsv
  Word pair counts computed from English sentences of Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org),
  licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), by
  tools/data/bigrams_from_tatoeba.py. Only counts are included, not sentences.
