DogBoard word data

en.lexicon.tsv (built by tools/data/build_lexicon.py) is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), because it contains frequencies from wordfreq.
It is built from:

  SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) 2020.12.07, http://wordlist.aspell.net/
    The collective work is Copyright 2000-2018 by Kevin Atkinson and the contributors listed in
    its copyright notice; used under that notice, included in full in licenses/SCOWL-Copyright.txt.
  wordfreq 3.1.1 word frequencies, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, data CC BY-SA 4.0,
    by Robyn Speer and contributors, which draw on Wikipedia, OpenSubtitles (via OPUS),
    SUBTLEX, NewsCrawl, GlobalVoices, Google Books Ngrams, Reddit and Twitter text.
  Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org), CC0: names of people, companies, brands, websites, apps.
  US Census Bureau name frequency lists (1990 first names, 2010 surnames), public domain.
  GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), CC BY 4.0: names of cities, countries and regions.
  Wikipedia "Lists of common misspellings/For machines", CC BY-SA 4.0: used only to leave
    misspellings out.

en.bigrams.tsv
  Word pair counts computed from English sentences of Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org),
  licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), by
  tools/data/bigrams_from_tatoeba.py. Only counts are included, not sentences.

en.charlm.tsv (tools/data/build_charlm.py) and en.ngrams.bin (built by tools/data/fineweb_sentences.py, KenLM lmplz and
  tools/data/arpa_to_dogboard.py)
  A letter model of words our dictionary lacks (en.charlm.tsv: probabilities only, no word list) and
  a word trigram language model (probabilities only, no text), both estimated from FineWeb
  (https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb, sample-10BT), by Hugging Face,
  licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0
  (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/); FineWeb is built from Common Crawl, whose
  terms of use also apply.

en.misspellings.txt (built by tools/data/build_misspellings.py)
  Misspelled forms from Wikipedia's "Lists of common misspellings/For machines",
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Lists_of_common_misspellings/For_machines, by Wikipedia
  contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); so this file is
  CC BY-SA 4.0. Only the misspelled forms our lexicon lacks are kept.

lm/v3.tflite, lm/v3.pieces.tsv (the neural language model and its word pieces)
  A small transformer (12 layers, width 384) trained by the DogBoard project on public text only;
  never on anything typed on a keyboard by DogBoard's users. It holds learned weights, not text.
  The training text, with thanks:
  Webis TLDR-17 (Reddit posts), Webis / Völske et al. 2017,
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/webis/tldr-17, CC BY 4.0.
  FineWeb (sample-10BT), Hugging Face, https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb,
    Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0; built from Common Crawl, whose terms of use
    also apply.
  SODA, Allen Institute for AI, https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/soda, CC BY 4.0.
  WildChat-1M (people's turns only), Allen Institute for AI,
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/WildChat-1M, ODC-By v1.0.
  Synthetic-Persona-Chat, Google, https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/Synthetic-Persona-Chat,
    CC BY 4.0.
  Leipzig Corpora Collection (English news 2024, English web 2018), Universität Leipzig,
    https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de, CC BY.
  Mozilla Common Voice sentences, CC0.
  Ubuntu IRC logs (irclogs.ubuntu.com), public domain.
  Made-up text messages and group chats written for this purpose by SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct
    (Hugging Face, Apache License 2.0) from invented prompts, without real people's messages.
  It runs on LiteRT (Google AI Edge, Apache License 2.0).

en.emoji.tsv, en.emoji-tones.tsv (built by tools/data/build_emoji_keywords.py)
  Emoji names and keywords, and skin-tone forms, from Unicode CLDR (common/annotations/en.xml,
  common/annotationsDerived/en.xml), Unicode License v3 (licenses/Unicode-License.txt); ranked by
  wordfreq 3.1.1 emoji frequencies (CC BY-SA 4.0), so en.emoji.tsv is CC BY-SA 4.0.

en.nosuggest.txt (built by tools/data/build_nosuggest.py)
  Words never offered by autocorrection, completion or prediction, from the List of Dirty,
  Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words (https://github.com/LDNOOBW), English list, CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); kept only where in DogBoard's lexicon.

clean-urls.json (fetched by tools/links/fetch_clean_urls.py)
  Brave's "Copy clean link" list (https://github.com/brave/adblock-lists, brave-lists/clean-urls.json),
  unchanged, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, included in licenses/Brave-clean-urls-MPL-2.0.txt.
  Used by Settings > Clipboard > Strip trackers from links.
