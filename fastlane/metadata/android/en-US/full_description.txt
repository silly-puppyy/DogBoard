<p><i>DogBoard</i> is a keyboard that fixes your typos the way you expect, and keeps everything you type on your phone. It has no internet permission at all.</p>
<p><b>Typing</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Autocorrect with a full English dictionary, a language model of word context, and a model of how fingers miss keys</li>
    <li>Looks back: the next word can fix the one before it</li>
    <li>Names, slang and brands you type stay as you typed them</li>
    <li>Learns your words and how you write, on the device; long-press a suggestion to remove it</li>
    <li>Text shortcuts, smart punctuation, emoji suggestions</li>
    <li>Space-bar trackpad to move the cursor</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Looks and feel</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Material You themes that follow your wallpaper, light and dark</li>
    <li>Adjustable keyboard height, number row, sound and vibration</li>
    <li>Clipboard history, one-handed and floating modes</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>No network access: nothing you type can leave the keyboard</li>
    <li>Incognito mode, and nothing learned in password fields</li>
    <li>Open source (Apache 2.0), built on FlorisBoard</li>
</ul>
<p>English (US) for now; more languages are planned.</p>
